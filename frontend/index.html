30 randomized windows · held-out day · lower cost is better

Each policy liquidates the same 25 BTC target inside a 300-tick window, drawn from 30 different random starting points on the held-out day. Bars show mean execution cost in basis points of arrival notional (implementation shortfall); whiskers show ±1 standard deviation.

One execution window, side by side

All five policies replay the identical historical window — same ticks, same order book — so differences come only from each policy's decisions. Lines that finish early hold flat (dashed) for the rest of the window.

No Trade omitted from this panel — its leftover penalty (−5,502 bps, applied once at window close) would flatten every other line to the axis.

Method. Order book reconstructed from Binance depth-diff events (top 10 levels/side). Reward = implementation shortfall vs. arrival midprice, minus a small continuous schedule-pacing penalty, minus a one-time leftover penalty for unsold inventory at window close. Temporary impact walks the real bid ladder; permanent impact shifts the ladder down per fill and decays ~3%/tick. Trained on three separate days (~45k ticks combined); scored here only on a fourth day held out entirely from training.
